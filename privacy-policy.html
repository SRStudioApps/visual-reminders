Visual Reminders

Overview

Visual Reminders is designed to keep your reminder library on your device. The free version does not require an app account. We do not operate a cloud reminder database, sell personal information, or use reminder content for advertising.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your reminder titles, notes, photos, links, categories, saved locations, alert schedules and preferences in local app storage. It uses this information to display, organize, edit and deliver reminders.

Location access

With your permission, Visual Reminders uses precise location, including background location when the app is closed or not in use, to detect visits to saved places and deliver location reminders. Quick Reminder captures a location when you take a photo. Location access is optional for reminders without a location. You can revoke it in Android Settings; location-based alerts may then stop working.

Online location and map services

Address and place searches use Geoapify. Search text and, when available, nearby coordinates are sent to Geoapify to return relevant suggestions. Geoapify may retain request information, including request content, IP address and timestamps, under its own privacy policy. Android geocoding, Google Play services for location and geofencing, and map services may process location or network information to provide their functions. Map requests may reveal the viewed area and IP address. These services are governed by their own policies. Reminder titles, notes and photos are not intentionally sent to these services as reminder content; text entered into address search is sent for that search.

Camera, photos and notifications

Photos are captured or selected at your request and stored with reminders. The app may save a separate personal copy of a camera photo in your gallery. Notifications and alarms use your saved settings to alert you. You control permissions in Android Settings; notification content may be visible on the lock screen depending on your device settings.

Backups, sharing and external apps

When you export a backup or share a reminder, you choose the destination or recipient. Files may contain private information and are not encrypted by the app. A selected cloud storage provider, recipient, or external app may retain a copy. Opening links launches another app or website subject to its own privacy practices. Android backup and gallery synchronization may also depend on device settings.

Retention and deletion

Reminder data remains on your device until you delete it or clear the app’s data or uninstall it, subject to Android backup and restore behavior. Archiving does not delete a reminder. Separately saved gallery photos, exported files, shared copies and device backups may remain and must be deleted separately. Third-party services retain information under their own policies. We cannot erase copies held by recipients or other providers.

Security and your choices

We use Android app storage and platform permission controls to protect reminder data. No storage system is completely secure. Keep your device locked and exported files private. You can use reminders without location access, manage permissions, delete reminders, and choose whether to export or share information.
